The Digital Paddy

AI-Based Pest Detection & Early Warning System

Bridging advanced Artificial Intelligence with Sri Lankan agriculture. Utilizing YOLOv8 edge-computing, IoT camera traps, and predictive weather modeling to safeguard local rice yields.

Explore Portfolio Documents

The Problem & Vision

Rice crops across regions like Ampara are highly vulnerable to localized pests and diseases, often leading to devastating yield losses before farmers can visually detect the threat. Traditional monitoring is labor-intensive, subjective, and strictly reactive.

The Digital Paddy proposes a proactive, real-time monitoring ecosystem. Validated alongside agricultural experts at the Rice Research and Development Institute (RRDI), Batalagoda And Rice Research Station in Sammanthurai, we deploy field-level IoT camera traps alongside high-resolution edge computing. By running optimized YOLOv8 object detection models integrated with local weather data APIs, we provide farming communities with an intelligent, highly accurate early warning system.

Core Technologies

  • YOLOv8 Edge AI

    High-speed, accurate pest classification from camera traps directly on localized edge devices, minimizing latency.

  • IoT Camera Traps

    Capturing real-time visual data and micro-climate metrics directly from the paddy fields.

  • Weather & API Integration

    Aggregating local field data with broader weather APIs for advanced predictive threat modeling.
